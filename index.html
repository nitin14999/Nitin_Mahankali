From sketch to data

The same four steps run through every project, from a test field in Greifswald to a laser cell in Dresden.

1 · Concept & safety

Understand the physics

Literature review, thermodynamics, hazard thinking. Ammonia and hydrogen don't forgive shortcuts.

2 · Engineering design

Make it buildable

PFDs and P&IDs, plot plans, equipment specification, CAD and design for additive manufacturing.

3 · Build & test

Measure what matters

Test rigs, instrumentation, commissioning support and DOE-based experimental campaigns.

4 · Analyse & model

Let the data decide

Python workflows, heat-release analysis, and equipment and plant models validated against experiments.

Beyond the CV

A mechanical engineer from Hyderabad who came to Germany for a master's degree in resource efficiency and stayed for the energy transition. The path runs from laser-melted titanium in Dresden, through fuel-cell systems at Sunfire, to green ammonia at Leibniz INP. Each step moved closer to the question that drives the work now: how to make ammonia a safe, practical carrier for renewable energy.

Happiest where the drawing meets the hardware: when a P&ID becomes a running rig, or a pressure trace explains why something behaves the way it does.

Currently building: dynamic equipment and plant-level models of the pilot plant in Python, energy-system modelling with PyPSA, and machine learning for engineering data.
