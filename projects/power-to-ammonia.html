Power-to-Ammonia & Ammonia-to-Power

Development and investigation of decentralized and modular ammonia-based energy systems for renewable energy integration and flexible energy conversion.

Project Overview

Development and investigation of decentralized and modular Power-to-Ammonia (PtA) and Ammonia-to-Power (A2P) systems for renewable energy integration and flexible energy conversion.

The work combines process engineering, thermodynamic analysis, mechanical system integration, equipment selection, experimental development and technical documentation.

Engineering Challenge

Renewable electricity generation is inherently variable. Integrating renewable power with chemical energy storage requires systems capable of operating under changing operating conditions.

Ammonia provides a potential energy carrier for storing and transporting renewable energy. The engineering challenge is therefore to develop compact, modular and flexible systems integrating hydrogen, nitrogen, ammonia synthesis, storage and downstream energy conversion.

Safety & Reliability

Particular attention is given to ammonia handling, pressure systems, gas handling, containment and operational safety.

Safety activities include HAZID, HAZOP, risk assessment, safety margins and consideration of material compatibility.

System-Level Concept

The system concept connects renewable electricity with hydrogen production, nitrogen supply, ammonia synthesis, storage and downstream power conversion.
