Ammonia Refueling System

Engineering and experimental development involving ammonia handling, storage, pressure systems, piping and conversion between pressure-liquefied and cold-liquefied ammonia concepts.

Project Overview

Engineering work related to ammonia refueling infrastructure and ammonia handling systems for energy applications.

The work involves process and mechanical engineering of systems handling ammonia under controlled pressure and temperature conditions, with particular attention to system integration, safety and operational requirements.

Ammonia Handling

Ammonia handling requires careful consideration of pressure, temperature, phase behaviour, material compatibility and containment.

Engineering activities therefore consider the requirements associated with both pressure-liquefied and cold-liquefied ammonia handling concepts.

Safety & Risk

  • Ammonia containment
  • Pressure safety
  • Material compatibility
  • Leak prevention
  • Ammonia detection
  • Operational safety
  • HAZID / HAZOP considerations
  • Safety margins

System Validation

Development activities include commissioning support, experimental validation and troubleshooting to verify system performance and resolve engineering issues.

Engineering Outcome

The work supports the development of ammonia refueling and handling infrastructure for emerging ammonia-based energy systems.

It combines process engineering, mechanical integration, pressure-system design, safety engineering and experimental validation.
