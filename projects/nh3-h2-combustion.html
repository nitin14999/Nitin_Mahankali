COMBUSTION · EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH

NH₃/H₂ Combustion & Engine Testing

Experimental investigation of ammonia-hydrogen combustion using an internal-combustion engine test bench, dynamometer testing, combustion mapping and emissions analysis.

Project Overview

Experimental research on ammonia-hydrogen combustion as an alternative fuel pathway for low-carbon power generation and mobility applications.

The work involved controlled combustion experiments using an IC-engine test bench coupled with a dynamometer, together with fuel-composition studies, combustion mapping and emissions measurements.

Research Challenge

Ammonia has potential as a carbon-free energy carrier and fuel, but its combustion characteristics differ significantly from conventional hydrocarbon fuels.

The experimental challenge is to understand how fuel composition, ignition conditions and mixture preparation influence combustion behaviour, engine performance, stability and emissions.

Experimental Setup

The experimental work was conducted using an internal-combustion engine test bench coupled to a dynamometer.
