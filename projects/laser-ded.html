Laser Directed Energy Deposition

Experimental development of Laser Directed Energy Deposition process parameters and materials characterization, with a focus on Ti-6Al-4V.

Project Overview

Experimental research on Laser Directed Energy Deposition (DED) with emphasis on process-parameter development, process windows, material characterization and process robustness.

The work was conducted during my research activities at Fraunhofer IWS and formed the basis of my Master's thesis.

Process Parameters

The experimental program investigated the influence of key process parameters on deposition behaviour and material quality.

  • Laser power
  • Powder/feed rate
  • Travel speed
  • Shielding gas conditions
  • Deposition strategy

Design of Experiments

Design-of-Experiments methods were used to systematically investigate parameter interactions and identify suitable processing conditions.

Process Qualification

The experimental approach included coupon builds, parameter studies and robustness analysis to support process-window development and qualification.

Engineering Outcome

The research established a systematic approach for relating laser DED process parameters to deposition behaviour, microstructure and material quality.

The work combined manufacturing-process engineering, materials science, experimental research and statistical data analysis.
