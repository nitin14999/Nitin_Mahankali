Ammonia Release Mitigation System

Development of an ammonia release mitigation concept combining containment, extraction, scrubbing and membrane-based ammonia capture.

Project Overview

Development of an Ammonia Release Mitigation System (ARMS) for capturing and treating ammonia released from a fuel cabinet.

The system combines extraction, water scrubbing and membrane contactor-based absorption to reduce ammonia emissions and provide controlled treatment of contaminated gas.

System Concept

The mitigation concept separates the primary water-scrubbing function from the acid-side membrane absorption loop.

Primary Capture Stage

The extraction system transfers ammonia-containing gas from the fuel cabinet to the scrubbing tank.

The water scrubbing stage provides the initial capture and treatment of ammonia before the remaining gas is routed toward the membrane contactor.

Membrane Contactor Stage

The membrane contactor provides a gas-liquid separation interface where ammonia is transferred from the gas phase into a circulating acid absorbent.

The process is driven by the ammonia partial-pressure difference between the gas and liquid sides of the membrane.

Engineering Outcome

The work establishes an integrated engineering concept for ammonia release detection and mitigation using multiple treatment stages.

The system also provides a platform for experimentally studying ammonia transfer and absorption using membrane contactor technology.
